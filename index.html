The product

One sensor. A complete picture of your soil.

Mazhi Sheti reads moisture, nutrients, pH, temperature and conductivity straight from the root zone — then turns those numbers into simple, timely actions for your farm.

7 parameters measured

  • Reads the root zone

    Three probes sit where your crop actually feeds, sampling every 15 minutes, all day, every day.

  • Speaks your language

    No spreadsheets. Just clear guidance: irrigate today, wait two days, add a little nitrogen.

  • Built for the field

    Weather-sealed, solar-friendly battery life and a signal that reaches from the far end of the plot.

How it works

From soil to decision in three steps.

  1. 01

    Plant the sensor

    Push Mazhi Sheti into the soil in under a minute. It pairs with the app and starts reading immediately.

  2. 02

    Watch the soil talk

    Moisture, NPK, pH, temperature and EC stream to your phone with trend lines for every parameter.

  3. 03

    Act with confidence

    Personalised recommendations tell you what to do, when to do it, and how much to save.

Features

Everything you need to grow smarter.

Real-time soil data

Live readings every 15 minutes, with history for the whole season.

AI-powered recommendations

Crop-aware advice on irrigation timing, fertiliser and soil health.

Mobile app access

Your whole farm in one pocket-sized dashboard, online or off.

Weather integration

Local forecasts woven into every recommendation, so rain never wastes a day.

Low power, long season

Weeks of runtime per charge, with a battery indicator that never surprises you.

Works offline
